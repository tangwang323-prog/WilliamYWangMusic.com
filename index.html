Biography

BornMarch 13, 2013
BirthplaceHancock, Michigan
Age13 years old
DisciplinePiano & Composition

William Yucheng Wang was born on March 13, 2013, in Hancock, Michigan — a small copper-country town on the Upper Peninsula.

From his earliest years, William displayed an extraordinary sensitivity to sound and an instinctive understanding of musical structure. He began piano studies at age four, and by seven had performed his first public recital to a standing ovation at a local music festival.

His development as both a performer and composer has been remarkably swift. Drawing on the rich tradition of the Western classical canon while weaving in influences from his Chinese heritage, William has forged a voice that is at once deeply rooted and strikingly original.

William continues to study, compose, and perform, with a growing profile that promises a career of enduring significance.
